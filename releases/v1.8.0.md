title: "v1.8.0: A page for every model, and head-to-head comparisons"
date: 2026-10-17
---
Every model now has its own page, and every pair of ranked models has a comparison page. No runs, data or scores changed.

## What's new
- **Model pages** at `systemonebench.dev/models/<model>/`, 20 in all: both indexes with their rank, calibration, latency and cost, then every text and image dataset with the model's accuracy, its rank, and the best score on the board. For example, [Decider V1.1 27B](https://systemonebench.dev/models/perplexity-decider-v1-1-27b/) and [Jev 1.14](https://systemonebench.dev/models/typesafe-jev-1-14/).
- **Comparison pages** at `systemonebench.dev/compare/<a>-vs-<b>/`, 120 in all, one for every pair of ranked models, with the better score marked on every line. For example, [Decider V1.1 27B vs Jev 1.14](https://systemonebench.dev/compare/perplexity-decider-v1-1-27b-vs-typesafe-jev-1-14/).
- The leaderboard links each model's details to its page, and `sitemap.xml` lists every page so search engines can find them.
- The pages are generated from the results every time the site deploys, so they always match the leaderboard.
