Round 7 runs eight open-weight models from the roadmap on all 3,250 text cases, from a 4-vCPU cloud server with no GPU. The data, the other runs and their scores are unchanged.

## New on the board
| Model | Maker | System One Index | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|
| d1-3B | Liquid AI | 78.4 | 10th of 24 |
| d3 nano | vLLM Semantic Router | 78.0 | 11th |
| d3 lite | vLLM Semantic Router | 74.9 | 15th |
| d3 edge | vLLM Semantic Router | 65.9 | 18th |
| d1-omni-600M | Liquid AI | 65.2 | 19th |
| Kev 0.8B | Jared Palmer | 63.6 | 21st |
| Naluri-308M | Jericho Siahaya | 61.0 | 22nd |
| Naluri-279M | Jericho Siahaya | 50.4 | 24th |

- **d1-3B is the first open-weight model in the top 10**, with d3 nano right behind it. Both place above Drex v1.5, Kev 4B and GPT-6 Luna Decisions at under 3 GB.
- d1-3B is also the second best calibrated model on the board (2.4%, behind Clef's 2.2%).
- The smallest models keep most of their English accuracy and lose much more in Arabic: d1-omni-600M is within 6 points of the full d1 in English and 30 below it in Arabic.
- The Naluri encoders score exactly what their model cards report. They are trained on the train splits of three datasets tested here; their notes on the site say which.
- The GGUF models ran on llama.cpp b11716 at Q8_0, the Naluri encoders on PyTorch. Every call in the published runs succeeded. Their latencies are local CPU timings, so they stay out of the latency charts.

## Roadmap
d1-3B, d1-omni-600M, d3 edge, lite and nano, Kev 0.8B and both Naluri encoders leave the roadmap. The larger d3 and Kev sizes stay listed.
