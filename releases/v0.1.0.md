The first runs, before the project became System One Bench: the open-weight Laya models against TypeSafe Jev 1.13, plus a live race of the two.

## What's in it
- `build_suites.py` and `build_arabic.py` build the case set every later release still uses: 3,250 cases (4,850 decisions) across 16 labelled datasets in English, French and Arabic.
- `score.py` scores accuracy and calibration (ECE, log loss, Brier) against each dataset's labels.
- `run_laya.py` runs Laya locally on an Apple M5 GPU; `run_jev.py` runs Jev 1.13 through the TypeSafe API.
- `server.py` and `race.html`: a live race of two models on the same cases.

## Results
- **Laya Router vs Jev 1.13 (2026-09-23)**, English and French datasets: Jev 75.5% against Laya's 51.0% over 3,650 decisions. Laya is 4 to 5 times faster on single-question calls (~89 ms against ~400 ms) and wins AG News, but falls well behind on SST-5, Banking77 and typed-decisions.
- **Laya Arabic fine-tunes (2026-09-25)**: laya-ara and laya-ara-rag lift the stock model a lot on the task families they trained on (mean of the 7 Arabic datasets 66.6 and 67.4 against 53.2), but Jev leads at 79.9.
