Round 9 runs the four decision models that joined OpenRouter's Decisions API since round 8 on all 3,250 text cases, and d1.1, the only one that reads images, on the vision track. The leaders don't change. The data, the other runs and their scores are unchanged.

## New on the board
| Model | Maker | System One Index | Rank | Vision Index | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mammoth 1 | Baseten | **82.9** | 4th of 34 | | |
| d1.1 | Liquid AI | 82.3 | 7th | **85.8** | 2nd of 12 |
| Elephant 1 | Baseten | 82.1 | 8th | | |
| Joey 1 | Baseten | 73.3 | 24th | | |

- **Mammoth 1 is 4th**, 0.04 points behind d1-ultra. Elephant 1 is 0.8 points behind it at about a third of the price. Round 8 left both out because they were then only on the free tier.
- **d1.1 is 0.4 points behind d1** on text, but better calibrated (2.1% against 2.9%) and the most accurate model on SST-2 (98.0%). On images it's 2nd, 0.3 points behind Span-02.
- **Joey 1 is the cheapest paid model on the board** ($0.004 per 1,000 calls). At 2B parameters it scores between Tev1 4B and Kev 4B.
- Baseten's three models don't read images. d1.1 doesn't answer Box questions.

## Latency
This round ran from a machine whose round trips don't compare with the cloud server behind the board's timings: a control run of Jev 1.14 took 492 ms median there against 136 ms on the board. So the four new models have no latency on the board (`latency_ms` is `null`) and stay out of the latency charts. Accuracy, calibration and cost don't depend on the network.

## Roadmap
Removed: Baseten's Mammoth 1 and Elephant 1, now on the board. Fastino GLiDE v1.2 leaves the watch list: its weights (26B, Fastino's community licence) have been on Hugging Face since 19 October, and the hosted GLiDE entry now notes them. The live race adds d1.1, Mammoth 1, Elephant 1 and Joey 1.
